Where your key lives, where requests go

There is no server behind DivanAI: the app is a single HTML file. That makes the privacy claim checkable instead of promised — there is nowhere for your key to be sent.

The request path

  1. You write a question; the app resolves which seat points at which service, in your browser.
  2. The request goes directly to that service.
  3. The answer comes back the same way and lands on the table.

No proxy, no logging layer, no relay of ours. The trade-off: the provider must allow browser requests (CORS). Services that do not allow it are simply not in the list.

The key

Stored in your browser's local storage and encrypted with AES-GCM. You can wipe it with one button in Settings, or choose not to save it at all on a shared machine.

What we do not claim

We do not claim the models are always right — they err. We do not control the providers' speed or quotas. And we promise no search ranking: the marketing for this app is the app itself.

Frequently asked questions

Can you see my key?

No. It is encrypted in your browser; there is no server of ours to read it.

Does it work offline?

The interface opens, but answers come from the provider, so no.

Can I self-host it?

Yes — one HTML file, Apache 2.0.
