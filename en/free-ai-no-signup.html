Free AI with no signup and no card

DivanAI costs nothing and asks for no account. But “free” means two different things: the app is free, and the service you talk to is free. Here they are, separately.

The app: zero

DivanAI is a single HTML file: no server, no database, no subscription, no ads, no analytics. There is no account to create. What you pay for the app: nothing.

The services: two routes

1) Keyless demo. Three seats on three free services, none of which asks for signup:

  • Pollinations → gpt-oss 20B
  • LLM7.io → Codestral
  • OVH AI Endpoints → Llama 3.3 70B

There is no published daily quota, but there are per-minute limits and busy periods. In our 7 November 2026 measurement the first keyless answer took 22 seconds; repeating the same question came back from cache in under a second. So: it works, it is slow.

2) Your own free key. Four services need no card and each shows up as one card in Settings → Connection: Google AI Studio, Groq, Z.ai (GLM) and OpenRouter. Each has a free tier; exact numbers live on the provider's own page, so we do not print them here.

Where money could appear

Three places: a free tier ran out and you moved to a paid model; you turned on OpenRouter web search (costs credits per request, off by default); or you used a paid image engine. All three are your choice — the app never switches to a paid path on its own.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it without a credit card?

Yes. The demo needs no signup; the four key-based services above need no card.

Is there a daily limit?

The app sets none. The services do; when one runs out, the seat says so and the table moves on.

Do you show ads?

No ads, no analytics, no cookie banner.
